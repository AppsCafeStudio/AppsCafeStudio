1. Introduction

Welcome to Chishtian Mart. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines how Chishtian Mart ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (Chishtian Mart: Daily Grocery), our website (https://chishtianmart.pk
), and our grocery delivery services in Chishtian, Punjab, Pakistan.

By downloading, installing, or using the Chishtian Mart application or placing an order through our platform, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly necessary to provide, fulfill, and improve our grocery delivery services.

A. Personal Information You Provide Directly
Full Name: To address you and verify your identity upon delivery.
Mobile Phone Number: Used as your unique account identifier, for order confirmation, delivery rider communication, and sending order status SMS notifications.
Delivery Address & Location Details: House number, street name, neighborhood/area, city (Chishtian), and nearby landmark to enable our delivery riders to find your address accurately.
Order & Transaction Details: Items purchased, quantities, timestamps, total amounts, and payment method (Cash on Delivery).
Customer Support Communications: Records and messages when you contact us via WhatsApp, phone, or email.
B. Technical & Device Information (Collected Automatically)
Device Attributes: Device model, manufacturer, and Android operating system version to ensure app compatibility and smooth performance.
Network & Connection Information: IP address, network connection type (Wi-Fi or cellular), and carrier information for network security and troubleshooting.
Push Notification Token (Firebase Cloud Messaging): An anonymous FCM registration token generated by Google Play Services to deliver real-time order status updates and dispatch notifications to your device.
Crash Reports & Performance Logs (Firebase Crashlytics): Anonymous crash stack traces and diagnostic data to quickly identify and resolve app bugs.
C. Information We DO NOT Collect
We DO NOT collect continuous background location.
We DO NOT access your photos, camera, microphone, or external storage.
We DO NOT read your contacts, call logs, or SMS messages.
We DO NOT collect credit card numbers, bank PINs, or financial secrets (All orders are Cash on Delivery).
3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational and customer service purposes:

Order Processing & Fulfillment: To process your cart, prepare your grocery basket, and deliver items to your specified doorstep in Chishtian within 30 to 45 minutes.
Rider Coordination: To assign your delivery to our nearest local rider and provide them with your address and contact number so they can reach you upon arrival.
Order Status Notifications: To send real-time push notifications and SMS updates regarding order confirmation, dispatch, and delivery completion.
Customer Support: To resolve complaints, item replacements, or refund requests when you contact our support desk.
App Maintenance & Reliability: To monitor application health, detect crashes, and improve overall performance across various Android devices.
Legal & Security Compliance: To prevent fraudulent orders, protect against malicious activity, and comply with applicable local laws in Pakistan.
4. How We Share Your Information

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies under any circumstances.

We only share data with the following authorized entities:

Delivery Riders: Our hired local delivery partners receive only the customer's name, phone number, delivery address, and ordered items list strictly for fulfilling the active delivery.
Cloud Infrastructure Providers:
Supabase Inc.: Secure cloud database hosting with industry-standard AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit.
Google Firebase (Google LLC): Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for push notifications and Firebase Crashlytics for anonymous stability diagnostics.
Cloudflare Inc.: Content Delivery Network (CDN) and DDoS protection to keep our services secure and fast.
Legal Authorities: Only if required by applicable Pakistani law, court order, or official law enforcement request to prevent fraud or illegal activities.
5. Data Security Safeguards

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse:

Encryption in Transit: All communication between the Chishtian Mart mobile application, website, and backend servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Cleartext HTTP traffic is completely disabled (usesCleartextTraffic="false").
Restricted Access: Access to order databases and customer records is strictly restricted to authenticated store managers and administrators using encrypted credentials.
No Unnecessary Permissions: The app requests only basic internet access and notification permissions, leaving your device sensors and files untouched.
6. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy
A. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill historical accounting records (such as completed order invoices for tax purposes).

B. User Right to Account & Data Deletion

Under Google Play's User Data Policy, you have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion:

Via Website Form: Visit our dedicated deletion portal at:
👉 https://chishtianmart.pk/delete-account

Enter your registered mobile phone number and submit the request.
Via Email: Send an email to privacy@chishtianmart.pk or support@chishtianmart.pk with the subject "Account Deletion Request" including your registered mobile number.
Via In-App: Open the app → Navigate to Account / Profile → Tap Delete My Account.

What Happens Upon Deletion:

Your personal name, mobile number, and saved delivery addresses are permanently purged from our active databases within 14 business days.
Historical order records are irreversibly anonymized (stripped of all personal identifying information) and retained solely for accounting and audit compliance.

8. Permissions Requested by the Android Application

The Chishtian Mart Android application requests only three standard, non-sensitive permissions:

android.permission.INTERNET: Allows the app to connect to our secure grocery catalog servers and submit orders.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the app to check whether your device has an active Wi-Fi or cellular internet connection.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Allows the app to show you live delivery notifications and order status alerts (Android 13+). You may enable or disable notifications at any time in your phone's system settings.
