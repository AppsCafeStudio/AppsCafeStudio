1. Introduction
Welcome to Chishtian Mart. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled in a safe, transparent, and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines how Chishtian Mart ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (Chishtian Mart: Daily Grocery) and our grocery delivery services in Chishtian, Punjab, Pakistan.
By downloading, installing, or using the Chishtian Mart application or placing an order through our platform, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
2. Information We Collect
We strictly follow the principle of data minimization and only collect information necessary to fulfill and improve our grocery delivery services.
•	Full Name: To address you respectfully and verify recipient identity upon delivery.
•	Mobile Phone Number: Used as your unique account identifier, for order confirmation, delivery rider communication, and sending transactional order status SMS alerts.
•	Delivery Address Details: House/plot number, street name, neighborhood/colony, city (Chishtian), and nearby landmark to enable our delivery riders to find your doorstep accurately.
•	Order & Transaction Details: Items purchased, quantities, order timestamps, total billed amounts, and payment method (Cash on Delivery).
•	Customer Support Communications: Records and messages when you contact us via WhatsApp or phone helpline.
•	Technical & Device Diagnostics: Device model, Android OS version, and anonymous push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging) to deliver real-time order status alerts.
What We Never Collect: We DO NOT track continuous background GPS location; we DO NOT access camera, photos, contacts, or storage; and we DO NOT collect banking passwords or payment card details. All orders are settled via Cash on Delivery (COD).
3. How We Use Your Information
•	Order Fulfillment: To pack, verify, and dispatch your grocery orders to your specified address in Chishtian.
•	Rider Delivery Coordination: To allow the assigned local delivery rider to call you when arriving at your doorstep.
•	Transactional Notifications: To send live push notifications and SMS updates regarding order confirmation, dispatch, and delivery completion.
•	Customer Support: To resolve inquiries, returns, or missing items via WhatsApp or phone helpline.
•	Fraud Prevention & Safety: To verify legitimate orders and prevent fraudulent spam activity.
4. How We Share Your Information
We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies under any circumstances.
•	Delivery Riders: Our hired local delivery partners receive only the customer's name, phone number, delivery address, and ordered items list strictly for fulfilling active orders.
•	SMS Gateway Partners: Your mobile phone number is processed through authorized telecommunication and SMS gateway providers in Pakistan solely for transmitting transactional order verification, rider dispatch, and delivery confirmation text messages. Phone numbers are never shared for advertising or third-party marketing.
•	Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Supabase Inc. (encrypted database storage) and Google Firebase (push notifications and crash diagnostics).
•	Legal Authorities: Only if strictly required under Pakistani law, valid court order, or official law enforcement request.
5. Data Security Safeguards
•	Encryption in Transit: All network communication is protected using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Cleartext traffic is completely blocked.
•	Encryption at Rest: Cloud databases are protected with industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
•	Access Controls: Administrative access is restricted strictly to authenticated store managers on a need-to-know basis.
6. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy
Under Google Play's User Data Policy, you have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time:
•	Via In-App: Open the app -> Navigate to Account / Profile -> Tap 'Delete My Account'.
•	Via WhatsApp / Phone Helpline: Call or message our helpline at +92 312 0417196 with your registered mobile phone number requesting account deletion.
Upon receiving your request, your personal name, mobile number, and saved delivery addresses are permanently purged from our active databases within 14 business days. Historical transaction totals are irreversibly anonymized for statutory tax auditing compliance.
7. Android Device Permissions
The app requests only standard non-sensitive permissions:
•	INTERNET: Allows the app to fetch real-time grocery prices and submit customer orders.
•	ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the app to detect network connectivity status and alert you when offline.
•	POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Allows optional delivery progress notifications on Android 13+ devices.
