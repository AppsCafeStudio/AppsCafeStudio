## 2. Information We Collect

We strictly follow the principle of data minimization and only collect information necessary to fulfill and improve our grocery delivery services.

### A. Personal Information You Provide Directly
* **Full Name:** To address you respectfully and verify recipient identity upon delivery.
* **Mobile Phone Number:** Used as your unique account identifier, for order confirmation, delivery rider communication, and sending transactional order status SMS alerts.
* **Delivery Address & Location Details:** House/plot number, street name, neighborhood/colony, city (Chishtian), and nearby landmark to enable our delivery riders to find your doorstep accurately.
* **Order & Transaction Details:** Items purchased, quantities, order timestamps, total billed amounts, and payment method (Cash on Delivery).
* **Customer Support Communications:** Records and messages when you contact us via WhatsApp or phone helpline.

### B. Technical & Device Information (Collected Automatically)
* **Device Attributes:** Device model, manufacturer, and Android operating system version to ensure app stability and smooth performance.
* **Network & Connection Information:** IP address, network connection status (Wi-Fi or cellular), and carrier information for network security and diagnostic logging.
* **Push Notification Token (Firebase Cloud Messaging):** An anonymous FCM registration token generated by Google Play Services to deliver real-time order status updates and dispatch notifications to your device.
* **Crash Reports & Performance Logs (Firebase Crashlytics):** Anonymous crash stack traces and diagnostic metrics to identify and fix technical issues.

### C. Information We DO NOT Collect
* **We DO NOT track continuous background location.**
* **We DO NOT access your photos, camera, microphone, or external storage.**
* **We DO NOT read your contacts, call logs, or personal SMS messages.**
* **We DO NOT collect credit card numbers, debit cards, or bank PINs** (All orders are fulfilled via Cash on Delivery).

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational and customer service purposes:
1. **Order Processing & Fulfillment:** To assemble, pack, and deliver your grocery orders to your specified address in Chishtian.
2. **Customer Communication & Delivery Coordination:** To allow our delivery rider to call you if clarification is needed regarding your address or arrival.
3. **Order Status Notifications:** To send real-time push notifications and transactional SMS updates regarding order confirmation, dispatch, and delivery completion.
4. **Customer Support:** To resolve complaints, item replacements, or refund requests when you contact our support desk.
5. **App Maintenance & Reliability:** To monitor application health, detect crashes, and improve overall performance across various Android devices.
6. **Legal & Security Compliance:** To prevent fraudulent orders, protect against malicious activity, and comply with applicable local laws in Pakistan.

## 4. How We Share Your Information

**We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies under any circumstances.**

We share minimal necessary data strictly with the following authorized entities:
* **Delivery Riders:** Our hired local delivery partners receive only the customer's name, phone number, delivery address, and ordered items list strictly for fulfilling the active delivery.
* **SMS Gateway Partners:** Your mobile phone number is processed through authorized telecommunication and SMS gateway providers in Pakistan solely for transmitting transactional order verification, rider dispatch, and delivery confirmation text messages. Phone numbers are never shared for advertising or third-party marketing.
* **Cloud Infrastructure Providers:**
  * **Supabase Inc.:** Secure cloud database hosting with industry-standard AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit.
  * **Google Firebase (Google LLC):** Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for push notifications and Firebase Crashlytics for anonymous stability diagnostics.
  * **Cloudflare Inc.:** Content Delivery Network (CDN) and DDoS protection to keep our services secure and fast.
* **Legal Authorities:** Only if required by applicable Pakistani law, court order, or official law enforcement request to prevent fraud or illegal activities.

## 5. Data Security Safeguards

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse:
* **Encryption in Transit:** All communication between the Chishtian Mart mobile application and backend servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (**TLS 1.3 / HTTPS**). Cleartext HTTP traffic is completely disabled (`usesCleartextTraffic="false"`).
* **Restricted Access:** Access to order databases and customer records is strictly restricted to authenticated store managers and administrators using encrypted credentials.
* **No Unnecessary Permissions:** The app requests only basic internet access and notification permissions, leaving your device sensors and files untouched.

## 6. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

### A. Data Retention
We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill historical accounting records (such as completed order invoices for tax purposes).

### B. User Right to Account & Data Deletion
Under Google Play's User Data Policy, you have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time.

**How to Request Account & Data Deletion:**
1. **Via In-App:** Open the app → Navigate to **Account / Profile** → Tap **Delete my data**.
2. **Via WhatsApp / Phone Helpline:** Contact our customer support team directly via phone or WhatsApp at **+92 312 0417196** with your registered mobile phone number requesting account deletion.

**What Happens Upon Deletion:**
* Your personal name, mobile number, and saved delivery addresses are permanently purged from our active databases within **14 business days**.
* Historical order records are irreversibly anonymized (stripped of all personal identifying information) and retained solely for accounting and audit compliance under Pakistani tax laws.

## 7. Android Device Permissions

The **Chishtian Mart** Android application requests only three standard, non-sensitive permissions:
1. `android.permission.INTERNET`: Allows the app to connect to our secure grocery catalog servers and submit orders.
2. `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Allows the app to check whether your device has an active Wi-Fi or cellular internet connection.
3. `android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: Allows the app to show you live delivery notifications and order status alerts (Android 13+). You may enable or disable notifications at any time in your phone's system settings.
